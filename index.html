How Pattern learns

A fresh LSTM learns to predict your next move from both players’ last 32 moves. It starts with random weights, so give it time to find a pattern.

After each reveal, two short AdamW updates learn from recent next-move predictions, giving newer rounds more weight. Weight decay and label smoothing reduce overconfidence. Learning stays on your device.

Pattern always chooses the move with the best expected score. Equally good moves are tied at random. Independent random choices cannot be reliably predicted.

Accuracy is measured before learning from each move. Chance is 33.3%.
